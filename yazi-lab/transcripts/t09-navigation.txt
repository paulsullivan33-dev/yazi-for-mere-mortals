Session: first look and moving around
Start directory: the lab's playground folder, holding three
folders (garden, kitchen, photos) and two files (budget.csv,
notes.txt). A sixth entry, .hidden-note.txt, is hidden by default.

How these screens were captured: the lab has no interactive
terminal, so Yazi ran on a pseudo-terminal (110x32) driven by a
small Python harness (ptyrun.py, kept in this folder). The harness
sent real keystrokes and rendered the terminal with the pyte
library. What you see is the rendered screen, trimmed vertically
where marked [...]. File results were then checked on disk with
ordinary shell commands, which are shown after each session.
The icons in the listings are Yazi's file-type glyphs, drawn from
a Nerd Font; in a plain font they show as boxes or blanks.
