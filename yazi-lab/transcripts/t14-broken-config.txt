Session: a configuration with a mistake in it
The lab's yazi.toml for this run said, in full:

  [mgr]
  show_hidden = tru

Started with that configuration, Yazi does not open. It prints
this, in full-screen text, before anything else (a garbage
string from the terminal probe has been left as it fell):

--- screen ---
Running with log level `yazi=DEBUG`, logs are written to ".../statebroken/yazi/yazi.log"
Failed to parse config ".../configbroken/yazi/yazi.toml"
Caused by:
TOML parse error at line 2, column 15
  |
2 | show_hidden = tru
  |               ^^^
invalid boolean, expected `true`
Press any key to continue with preset settings...
--- end screen ---

Pressing a key starts Yazi with its built-in preset settings,
so a typo costs you your configuration, never the program.
